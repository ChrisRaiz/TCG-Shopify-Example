Frequently asked questions

How do I buy a card?

Every card links to its eBay listing. Checkout, payment, and buyer protection all run through eBay.

How does the bundle discount work?

Add eligible items to your cart: 2 items get 10% off, 3 get 15% off, and 4 or more get 20% off. Each listing shows whether it qualifies.

How fast do orders ship?

Orders ship within 2 business days of purchase.

What do the conditions mean?

Raw cards are listed as Near Mint, Lightly Played and so on. Graded cards are slabbed by PSA or CGC. Check the listing photos and condition notes before you buy.

How do I reach you?

Message us through eBay with the Contact button. We're happy to help with any card.
